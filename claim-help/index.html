CLAIM HELP

A repair plan.
A clearer next step.

If your RV has been damaged, start by gathering the details and calling our shop to discuss a damage evaluation.

GETTING STARTED

From the first call
to the repair.

  1. Gather your information

    Have your RV’s year, make, model, and VIN available, along with photos of the damage and any estimate already prepared.

  2. Contact your insurance company

    Report the incident and obtain your claim number and adjuster contact information. Ask your insurer about the next steps for your claim.

  3. Call AAA RV

    We will discuss your damage evaluation, repair plans, and appointment details. Your estimate is provided at no charge when AAA RV completes the approved repairs.

  4. Review the repair plan

    Our team can work with your adjuster on repair details. Coverage decisions belong to the insurer; repair authorization and scheduling are discussed with you.

Have these details ready.

  • Your name and contact number
  • RV year, make, model, and VIN
  • Insurance company and claim number
  • Adjuster name and contact information
  • Photos and existing repair estimates
  • Incident date and a description of the damage
YOUR ESTIMATE & REPAIRS

No estimate fee when AAA RV completes your approved repairs.

When AAA RV completes the repairs in your approved estimate, there is no estimate charge. If we prepare an estimate and you choose not to have those approved repairs completed at AAA RV, an estimate fee applies.
