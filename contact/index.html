Our estimate
policy.

Know what to expect before you bring your RV in.

DAMAGE EVALUATIONS

Never pay for an estimate when you have your repairs done at AAA RV.

When AAA RV completes the repairs in your approved estimate, there is no estimate charge. If we prepare an estimate and you choose not to have those approved repairs completed at AAA RV, an estimate fee applies.

For example, an estimate fee applies if you choose to keep the repair funds or have the repairs completed elsewhere. Call us to discuss your plans and any applicable fee before your evaluation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before your visit.

Will I pay for an estimate if AAA RV repairs my RV?

No. When AAA RV completes the repairs in your approved estimate, there is no estimate charge.

When does an estimate fee apply?

An estimate fee applies if we prepare an estimate and you choose not to have the approved repairs completed at AAA RV, such as keeping the repair funds or having the work done elsewhere. Call us to discuss the applicable fee before your evaluation.

What qualifies for a free quote?

Free quotes are available for service and maintenance related items. Finding the cause of an unknown problem may require diagnostic labor.

Do I need to call before bringing my RV?

Please call the shop to arrange your appointment or drop-off and confirm what information to bring.

Do you work on motorhomes and towable RVs?

We work on motorhomes, fifth wheels, and travel trailers. Motorhome engine and transmission repairs are outside our service scope.

Can you help with an insurance repair?

Yes. Bring your claim number, adjuster contact information, and any estimate or photos you already have. We can discuss the evaluation and repair process with you.
